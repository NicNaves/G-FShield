The X-CANIDS replication extended this evidence to two scenarios with 688 predictors and a further 120 paired runs. G-FShield again overlapped more than 99.65\% of local search with construction and performed approximately 2.3 times as many IWSSR evaluations per second, but the monolith retained better anytime AUC and lower consumption. Under a common XGBoost and held-out test, the external result depended on the attack: for fabrication, G-FShield median macro-F1 was 0.887680 versus 0.882423 for the historical IWSHAP subset, with 26 of 30 runs above the comparator; for suspension, it was 0.723518 versus 0.830048 and no run exceeded it. Even for fabrication, G-FShield used a median 17 features rather than four and traded recall for precision. Thus, there is a descriptive F1 advantage only in that scenario, not general superiority over IWSHAP.
